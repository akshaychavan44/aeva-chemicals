The chemistry underneath

Right chemistry. No clashes.

Specialty chemicals share the same bath, tank or mix, so they have to work together.

By ionic charge

AnionicCationicNon-ionicAmphoteric

By chemical base

SurfactantsPolymersEnzymesSiliconesResinsFluorochemicalsInorganics

Why it matters

A cationic product can react with anionic residues already in the system, whether that's a softener on fabric or a flocculant in water. We match the chemistry across your process, so every step works with the next.
