Our story

Small doses. Big differences.

Specialty chemicals are rarely the biggest item on a bill of materials, but they decide how a product performs: how evenly a fabric dyes, how a shampoo foams, how a paint flows, how clean water runs, how strong paper is, how concrete sets.

Between us, we've spent more than 50 years in specialty chemicals. We kept seeing good products fall short for small reasons, and almost every time, the fix was the right chemistry.

So we built Aeva around one simple idea: get the chemistry right, and everything after it gets easier. Today we serve manufacturers across six industries, in India and five export markets, backed by our own R&D and a plant near Mumbai producing over 20 tonnes a day.

Our mission

Our vision

Better products and less waste, wherever things are made.
