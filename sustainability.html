Our circular approach

Each step makes the next one cleaner.

From the formula to the finished product, four commitments feed into each other.

01

Cleaner formulations

APEO-free and formaldehyde-free chemistry, safer for the people who make and use the products.

02

Built for compliance

Developed in line with global restricted-substance standards, with compliance documents available on request.

03

Right first time

Consistent grades that work batch after batch, so plants avoid costly reworks.

04

Less water, energy & waste

Fewer reworks and baths mean less water, heating and effluent on every line.

Sustainability in practice

Where the savings show up.

Lower-temperature wash-off

Our soaping agent washes loose dye off reactive-dyed cotton at lower temperatures, so mills heat less water.

Fewer baths

Cleaner wash-off in fewer baths saves water and cuts the effluent a plant has to treat.

Made for your process

Grades tuned to each plant's water, equipment and raw materials, so processes run the way they should.

Tested before dispatch

Every batch is tested in our lab before it leaves, so what you trial is what you get in bulk.
